## Concept

Responsible AI (RAI) turns AI risk into explicit design, validation, and operations controls. Lab 2 requires alignment to Responsible AI Standard V2 language and Customer Impact Assessment notes.

## Required evidence themes

- Fairness.
- Reliability and safety.
- Privacy and security.
- Inclusiveness.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Human oversight and escalation.
- Monitoring and continuous improvement.

## Classroom guidance

Ask learners to write harms in plain language. A good mitigation is testable, owned, and traceable to operations. A statement like "we use RAI" is not acceptable evidence.
