# Contoso Support Policy

Contoso provides fictional support levels for the MicroHack training scenario.
The support desk is the first contact point for all assistant issues.

## Support Hours

Standard support runs from 08:00 to 18:00 local business time, Monday through
Friday, excluding fictional Contoso holidays. Critical incidents can be
escalated to on-call support at any time during the pilot.

## Incident Severity

Severity 1 means the assistant is unavailable for all pilot users or produces a
confirmed unsafe response. Severity 2 means a major feature is degraded or a
business workflow is blocked. Severity 3 means a minor issue has a workaround.

## Escalation

The service desk triages issues, records impact, and escalates Severity 1 and
Severity 2 incidents to the BAU operations team. Security or privacy concerns
must also be routed to the fictional Contoso security owner.

## User Feedback

Pilot users can submit feedback for incorrect, missing, or unclear answers. The
product owner reviews feedback weekly and adds approved changes to the backlog.
