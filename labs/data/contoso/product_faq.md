# Contoso Product FAQ

Contoso is a fictional enterprise that provides productivity software for
manufacturing, retail, and professional services customers. The training
scenario uses anonymized customers named Customer A, Customer B, and Customer C.

## Products

Contoso Insight Hub is a knowledge assistant that helps employees find approved
product, support, security, and service-level information. It answers questions
only from approved source documents and should cite the source used.

Contoso Field Companion is a mobile-friendly assistant for field teams. It
summarizes service procedures, product capabilities, and escalation paths. It is
not approved for legal, financial, medical, or safety-critical advice.

## Usage Guidance

Employees should ask specific questions and check citations before acting on an
answer. If the assistant cannot find grounded information, it should say that it
does not know and direct the user to the service desk.

## Supported Languages

The fictional pilot supports English for the first release. Additional languages
require separate evaluation and accessibility review.
