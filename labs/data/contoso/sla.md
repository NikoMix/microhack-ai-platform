# Contoso Service-Level Agreement

This fictional SLA is for training only. It supports the Module B evidence
templates and evaluation lab.

## Availability

The pilot target availability is 99.5 percent during standard support hours.
Production target availability is 99.9 percent after BAU acceptance.

## Performance

The target response time for common grounded questions is a p95 latency of five
seconds or less during the pilot. Batch indexing jobs should complete within two
hours for the pilot data set.

## Recovery

The recovery time objective for the pilot is four hours. The recovery point
objective for configuration and approved source documents is one business day.

## Review Cadence

Service health, costs, incidents, and evaluation trends are reviewed weekly
during the four-week hypercare period. After hypercare, the BAU operations team
reviews the service monthly.
